Greater Des Moines

Good food gets thrown out every night. Families nearby go without.

Xenia connects the two. A store or restaurant posts its extra food in one sentence. Xenia finds the pantry that can safely take it, and neighbors see where to pick it up. Always free.

Xenia (ZEE-nee-uh): the ancient Greek custom of feeding whoever comes to your door.

  1. A store posts it"40 vegetarian meals, refrigerated, need gone by 7pm." That is the whole form.
  2. Xenia finds the pantry that asked for itPantries post what they're short on. Xenia rules out places that can't store the food or aren't open in time, then ranks the rest by need and distance.
  3. Neighbors pick it upThe food shows up below with where and when. No sign-up, no ID, no cost.

What happens next

Xenia checks eight pantries and kitchens. You'll see which ones can take your food, which can't and why, and the score behind the top pick.

What pantries need

Most food-rescue tools only let the giver speak. Here pantries ask first, and each donation counts toward the request it answers.

Give time

Food doesn't move itself. Stores, pantries and the city post jobs that need hands. Anyone can take one. Nobody has to work to eat.

What is real here

The pantry list, phone numbers and hours are real and public. The stores, the postings and the eight pantries in the matching demo are made-up examples. No partnership with any named organization is claimed.
