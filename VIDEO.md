# FoodLink — 3-minute demo video script

Film on a phone + screen recording. All data is simulated — say so once on camera (it's also on every screen).
Live app: https://firebreathingunicorn.github.io/foodlink-dsm/

| # | Time | On screen | Say (or paraphrase) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0:00–0:20 | Home screen | "In Polk County, 60,000 of our neighbors are food insecure — and USDA says a third of America's food is thrown away. The food exists. The people exist. The connection doesn't. This is FoodLink — the real-time food grid for Greater Des Moines." |
| 2 | 0:20–0:50 | Type the donation: *"40 vegetarian prepared meals, refrigerated, need gone by 7pm"* | "I'm a grocery manager with surplus. I don't fill forms — I type one sentence, like a text. Watch the AI read it: food type, quantity, storage, diet, deadline. I check safe handling. Done." |
| 3 | 0:50–1:25 | Match screen — run your finger down the bars, then the gated org | "Eight organizations checked in milliseconds. Four fail hard safety gates — and you can see exactly why: 'no reheat capacity.' The winner scores 82.4 — and the math is *on screen*: urgency, distance, capacity, transport. We stress-tested this ranking with 100,000 random weightings — #1 holds in ~100% of them. We didn't tune the weights. We proved the ranking survives them." |
| 4 | 1:25–1:45 | Scroll to *Direct to a nearby household* (FDIA badge) → tap Connect → receipt | "And because the 2023 Food Donation Improvement Act protects donors giving straight to families, hot meals can skip the warehouse and go direct — warm shelf to warm table. Every donation closes with an impact receipt: 48 pounds, $140.80 in value, a potential $3,660-a-year deduction." |
| 5 | 1:45–2:15 | Switch to Find food → Español toggle → cards | "Now the other side. A household opens FoodLink — no login, no documents, no stigma. In Spanish. Real-time, honest cards: what's available, where, which window — confirmed by the receiving organization. Cards expire on their own, so no one chases food that's gone." |
| 6 | 2:15–2:45 | Assistant: tap quick chip *"¿Hay vegetales?"* → reply; show #how briefly | "And the 34% of low-income neighbors who are smartphone-only? Text FOOD. The assistant answers in your language, grounded in the live inventory — never inventing food, never promising eligibility. The exact prompt is published, in the app." *(If AI configured, this is live. If not: "running in offline mode right now — flip to the template layer.")* |
| 7 | 2:45–3:00 | Needs Board + pulse | "Pantries finally get a voice — 'we need 50 meals Saturday' — and the network exports its own open data. FoodLink doesn't create more food. It makes the food that already exists *easier to reach*." |

## Recording notes
- Configure AI before filming (⚙️ → Ollama/gemma3 or a free Groq key) so beat 6 is live.
- Type the donation sentence live — don't pre-fill it; the parse animation is the moment.
- If recording fails: the 8 beats above map 1:1 to the live demo order — the app IS the storyboard.
- End card (3 s): FoodLink · Hack Away Hunger · team names.
